02

Set it up on Whop

Whop is free to start — it only takes a cut when you sell (~2.7% + $0.30 per sale). You'll make two products.

  1. Create your Whop (your store) and a seller account. Pick a clean, searchable name — your storefront, not a brand nobody knows yet.
  2. Make the paid product — "The Reseller Profit Kit." Use Whop's Files app to upload the deliverables so buyers download them after paying. Set price to a one-time $12.
  3. Make the free product — "Margin Tracker (Free)." Attach just the tracker file, set price to $0. This is the lead magnet.
  4. Write the listings using the copy in section 03. Add clean screenshots of the tracker's dashboard and the fee tables — buyers need to see it.
  5. Test as a buyer. Buy your own free product, download it, confirm every file opens. A broken download is a refund and a bad review.
  6. Publish and grab both links. The free link is what you share everywhere; it funnels to the paid one.
Delivery format: ship the tracker as the .html file (opens in any browser, no install) and the three guides as PDFs so they're easy to read and keep. Whop's access control gates who can download, but can't stop a file being copied — so your edge is the quality, the bundle, and free updates, not locking the file down. Whop's exact menus change, so follow their current Files-app prompts as you go.

Getting buyers with $0

You can't buy ads and you've never marketed. Fine — none of the channels below cost money. The whole game: give the free tracker genuinely, let it earn trust, and let trust sell the Kit.

Channels you fully control (start here)

  • TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels. Post your demo clips + short "how much eBay really takes" value videos. You own the posting, so no spam rules — this is your best no-audience start. Link the free tracker in bio.

06

The "don't get banned" rules

Communities are the fastest free traffic — and the fastest way to get yourself banned if you treat them like billboards. Follow these:

  • Never drop a paid link cold. Read each community's self-promo rules first — many ban it outright.
  • Give 10× more than you ask. Answer dozens of questions for every soft mention of your tool.
  • Lead with free, always. The free tracker is welcome where a sales link gets removed.
  • Be a real member, not an account that only posts links. Mods can tell instantly.
  • Don't copy-paste the same message everywhere. It reads as spam and gets flagged.

Your first 30 days

  1. Days 1–3: Export the guides to PDF, set up both Whop products, test the downloads as a buyer.
  2. Days 3–5: Record 5–10 demo clips. Take clean screenshots for the listing and Pinterest.
  3. Week 1: Post your first clips on TikTok/Shorts/Reels. Set up Pinterest pins. Make your Whop title searchable.
  4. Week 2: Join the communities. Just help — no links yet. Build a little trust and karma.
  5. Week 3: Keep posting clips (double down on whatever got views). Start sharing the free tracker where it genuinely helps.
  6. Week 4: Ask every happy free user for a review. Reviews are the unlock — once you have a handful, raise the Kit toward $19.
08

Honest expectations

First sales are slow — that's normal, not failure. With no audience, your first week might be a handful of free downloads and zero paid sales. That's fine. The free tracker is seeding trust that pays off over weeks, not hours.

Consistency beats everything. The person who posts a clip a day for a month wins over the one who posts ten in a day and quits.

  • Win #1: first free downloads → people are finding you.
  • Win #2: first paid sale → the offer works.
  • Win #3: first review → now you can raise the price and compound.

Keep improving the product from real feedback, keep posting, keep helping. A small, genuinely useful product that people vouch for beats a flashy one nobody trusts. You've already built the hard part — now it's reps.
