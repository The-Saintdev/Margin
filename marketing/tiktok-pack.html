05

Posting rules that actually matter

  • Post 1 a day for 30 days. Volume + consistency beats perfection. The algorithm rewards showing up.
  • Hook in the first 2 seconds — spoken and on-screen text. No slow intros, no "hey guys."
  • Film vertical, good light, keep it under 30s to start. Fast cuts hold attention.
  • Post natively to each app (upload the file), don't just share a TikTok link to Reels — each platform buries outside links.
  • Reply to every comment — comments boost reach, and "what's the tool?" is a buyer.
  • Double down on winners. When one hook pops, make five more like it.
  • CTA to bio, not a link in-video. "Free tracker in my bio" — keep the paid link one step away.
